# Roadmap process

## Where the roadmap lives

The roadmap is a GitHub Project on the Morton-Labs-AI organization:
https://github.com/orgs/Morton-Labs-AI/projects

It has three columns: **Now**, **Next**, **Later**. Each card links to an
issue or RFC. If it is not on the board, it is not on the roadmap.

## How things get on it

- Anyone can propose an item by opening an issue or Discussion.
- A maintainer adds it to **Later** when it is clear and in scope.
- Items move to **Next** and **Now** at the quarterly review.
- Breaking changes need an accepted RFC before they enter **Now**.

## Quarterly review

Maintainers review the board in the first two weeks of January, April,
July, and October. The review:

1. Closes or archives items that shipped.
2. Moves items between columns.
3. Drops items nobody has touched in two quarters, with a note on the
   issue.
4. Posts a short summary in Discussions.

The steward schedules the review until a Steering Committee exists.

## What the roadmap is not

It is not a promise or a release date. The open-source project has no SLA
(see SUPPORT.md). Hosted-service plans may run ahead of the public board.
