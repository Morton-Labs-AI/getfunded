# Organization enrichment — 2026-10-17

This is an initial research pass, not completion of the requested whole-database
review. The live corpus contains **2,309,073 organizations**. This work performed
an aggregate quality census over that population and website/discovery research
on **109 distinct organizations**. **2,308,964 organizations remain outside this
research campaign.** None of the campaign's profiles has been human-reviewed.

The original `open-funder-db` repository directs development to this maintained
`getfunded/corpus` copy. The proposed code and research compilation belong here.

## What changed

- Added an exhaustive inventory option and a resumable, private research queue.
  Missing websites and existing confirmed profiles stay in the inventory.
- Added a robots-aware crawler with bounded public HTTP requests, redirect/IP
  checks, TLS validation, topic-based link discovery and hashed private evidence.
- Wrote the [research protocol](ORGANIZATION-RESEARCH.md), including program and
  cycle scope, reverse research, conflict handling and publication gates.
- Registered **12 first-party source definitions** in the live database: ten
  HTML indexes, one RSS feed and one manual source. All twelve link to existing
  organizations. No new scheduler was installed.
- Added **196 candidate announcement URLs**, linked to existing organizations.
  These are discoveries awaiting article review, not 196 verified funding
  opportunities. **Zero signals were published.**
- Compiled **18 original research notes** and **15 attributed website candidates**
  for review. They do not overwrite registry websites or confirmed profiles.

The sources and URLs use the existing signals ingestion path and licensing
category. Raw file IDs **462–463** precede their ingestions; ledger runs **548–549**
record the completed loads. The CSVs are original compilations of public URLs and
identifiers. Publisher HTML and excerpts remain in ignored local storage with
`publisher_website`, nonrepublishable status.

The cohort combines sixteen science-relevant funders with the hundred private
foundations ranked highest in the queried latest qualifying distributions; seven

triage measure, not cash grants available to outside applicants.

The organization passes acquired **422 HTML pages** across **405 unique URLs**.
Separate acquisition added twelve news index pages, one recipient announcement
and one RSS feed: **435 HTML fetches and one RSS response** in total. Shared sites

leave unvisited links and are not proof of research completeness.

The broader cohort initially had 25 missing websites. Search produced fifteen
candidate surfaces, some belonging to a parent or subsidiary rather than the
legal entity itself. Ten remain unresolved. An inaccessible site or unsuccessful
search never becomes a `closed`, `inactive` or `no website exists` assertion.

- **Packard:** its evergreen fellowship page and 2026 announcement state different
  award amounts. Keep cycle-specific assertions; the announcement does not state
  the next cycle's exact amount. [Program](https://www.packard.org/approach/fellowships-for-science-engineering/),
  [2026 announcement](https://www.packard.org/insights/news/the-david-and-lucile-packard-foundation-announces-the-2026-class-of-packard-fellows-for-science-and-engineering/).

  3 p.m. Eastern deadline and new platform instructions. [Current instructions](https://www.jccfund.org/eligibilty-criteria/important-dates-and-documents/).
- **O'Donnell:** a recipient's October 15 announcement describes a final gift of
  remaining assets. Flag possible spend-down implications; do not infer legal
  dissolution. [UT Southwestern announcement](https://www.utsouthwestern.edu/newsroom/articles/year-2026/oct-odonnell-foundation-gift.html).
- **Schmidt:** the subsidiary's FAQ identifies the strategic-innovation fund by
  EIN. This supports an attributed relationship, not merging the entities.
  [Schmidt Sciences FAQ](https://www.schmidtsciences.org/faq/).
- **McDonnell:** its current mission centers on St. Louis economic opportunity;
  historical science awards alone are weak evidence of present fit.
  [Current mission](https://www.jsmf.org/our-mission/).

Other review notes distinguish HHMI competitions from unsolicited requests,
Templeton's closed 2026 window from its evergreen application process, Beckman's
indirect-cost policy, Keck's revised instructions and patient-assistance

## Outstanding work and limits

The local shell has no `DATABASE_URL`. Live census and candidate ingestion used
the connected database tool. The full streaming inventory command has **not**
been executed against the entire database. Its SQL was exercised on one live
organization. A separate global website-coverage query exceeded its time budget;
no overall website count is claimed.

Full-population research needs the inventory export, missing-site discovery,
per-entity website/news/reverse research and review of supported claims. The
current crawler does not implement automated search, PDF extraction, JavaScript
rendering, sitemap traversal or grant-API pagination. The protocol names those
future adapters explicitly. Ten unresolved websites and seventeen acquisition
blockers are concrete follow-up work within this cohort alone.

MacArthur's legacy index behaves like a soft 404, Keck's index exposes no article
links in static HTML, and McDonnell's broad root-path matching included navigation.
The compilation leaves Keck manual, uses McDonnell's verified RSS, and does not
enable the broken MacArthur route. The existing standalone signals poller is a
separate fetch implementation; this PR does not claim to retrofit its network
behavior with the new crawler.

The proposed next phase is a complete inventory-backed campaign plus targeted
adapters and claim review, with the same denominator retained throughout.
No whole-database completion, autonomous background job or human approval is
implied by this report.
