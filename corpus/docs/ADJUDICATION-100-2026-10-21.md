The disagreement rate, source-scope changes and additional misses within the second pass are too large to support automatic qualification or cheaper-model promotion.

This fulfills the source-adjudication work as a separate reading and targeted evidence-resolution pass. It does **not** establish independent human accuracy: the same agent performed both passes, retained prior campaign context, and selected a challenge-enriched sample from previously reviewed organizations. Earlier per-case labels were omitted from the reading cards and the new labels were hash-sealed before comparison. The sampler necessarily used old labels for stratification. Reported agreement is repeatability under this procedure, not a blind external gold standard, population accuracy, precision or recall. Some disagreements reflect a different supported program/recipient scope rather than a demonstrably wrong earlier statement.

## Rules to implement before another scale attempt

| If evidence shows… | Required decision |
|---|---|
| Exact EIN on a source, but multiple named entities | Record publisher association; hold target-specific activities until the relevant legal entity is identified. |
| Two supported programs or recipient types | Store program-level components rather than force one organization-wide role. |
| Software, equipment, commodities or own-service fee discounts | Keep in-kind/internal subsidy separate from outgoing cash grants. |
| An incoming award, third-party quote, resource list or unanswered FAQ heading | Do not establish target grantmaking from it. |
| An open headline with a past deadline | Flag contradictory/expired cycle evidence; do not admit an open-current claim. |
| A future opening or prerequisite LOI stage | Show the future date or prerequisite, not a currently usable unrestricted application. |
| A pause, invitation-only policy or program-specific restriction | Apply it within its named program; generic forms do not override it. |
| A generic contact address | Keep contact available without inventing a grant-inquiry invitation. |
| A bare annual deadline without a year | Retain it as annual-date evidence; current-cycle status remains unresolved. |
| Fresh HTTP200 HTML | Record access observation only; site identity, substantive content and whole-site update date remain distinct. |

These are proposed decision requirements derived from reviewed development evidence, not newly validated production rules. Next implement a small program-level structure and full-policy/negation/date retrieval on this development set. Then use an untouched, separately adjudicated reference set and actual invoice receipts for the accuracy/cost gate. This 100-case sample is now development material and cannot be reused as a fresh accuracy holdout. External reviewer labels remain the prerequisite for independent semantic accuracy.
